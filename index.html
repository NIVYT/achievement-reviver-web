In Minecraft Bedrock Edition, toggling cheats or opening a world in Creative mode irreversibly marks tags inside level.dat. This disables Xbox Achievements for that world forever.

In-Memory Zero-Copy Patching

Achievement Reviver reads the binary stream directly in your browser using DataView and modifies the exact byte values in place. No re-serialization and zero risk of tag order corruption.

level.dat_old Synchronization

Bedrock keeps a backup level.dat_old file. Both primary and backup files are inspected and patched so the game never rolls back your achievement status.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your gigabyte-sized world files never leave your computer. All processing happens entirely inside your web browser using WebAssembly and native TypedArrays.

Will I get banned on Xbox Live for doing this? +
No. Achievement eligibility is checked purely by the game client via local world NBT flags. This tool restores your world save to standard Survival specifications — no external hacks or memory injectors are used.
Will this affect my builds, inventory, or chunks? +
Not at all. The chunk database (LevelDB), player inventory, entities, and block states remain 100% untouched. Only the 5 specific cheat/gametype tags in level.dat are updated.
What if my world archive is over 100 MB? +
Achievement Reviver easily handles large world archives up to 300+ MB directly in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox thanks to high-speed stream reading and selective ZIP patching.
